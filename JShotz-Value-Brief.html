JShotz browser workflow evidence

Turn browser work into trusted evidence

JShotz records a web workflow as ordered screenshots, then creates shareable PDF or Word evidence without the manual screenshot-and-document assembly cycle.

30 min illustrative saving per 20-step evidence pack

The problem: QA, support, operations, and delivery teams often need to prove what happened in a browser. Today they re-run the process, take screenshots one by one, paste and label them in a document, and repair gaps when the action, page title, URL, or screenshot sequence does not match.

Where to use JShotz

  • QA and UAT evidence for a completed test flow
  • Support tickets and incident reproduction steps
  • Release validation, sign-off, and audit evidence
  • Customer or internal how-to walkthroughs
  • API-aware defect triage when browser network context matters

Benefits

  • Captures click, scroll, modal, and linked-tab steps in action order
  • Binds each frame to the rendered page title and URL
  • Exports selected frames and notes directly to PDF or Word
  • Supports checkpoints, resumed sessions, and regenerated evidence
  • Reduces missed steps, rework, and ambiguity during reviews
